01 / RESEARCH

Research context

Cosmological simulations generate large datasets describing galaxies in different physical environments and under different model parameters. These data provide an opportunity to study how galaxy properties vary, and a challenge: a statistical model must capture relationships across several variables while retaining the features important to a scientific comparison.

In a research tutorial supervised by Professor Paul Torrey, I worked with the DREAMS simulation suite and conditional normalizing flows to model distributions of galaxy properties. I was interested in what the trained model could reproduce from the original simulation data and how its output could be evaluated.

02 / RESEARCH

Data, code, and computing

I prepared simulation data for the modeling workflow, adapted existing Python research code, and organized experiments with different training configurations. A conditional normalizing flow models a probability distribution while incorporating conditioning variables, allowing the generated distribution to change with the specified inputs.

I ran training and analysis jobs on UVA's Rivanna high-performance computing cluster. This involved working with a shared research environment, managing datasets and experiment configurations, and saving outputs for comparison across runs.

03 / RESEARCH

Comparing model outputs

I compared distributions sampled from the trained models with distributions from the underlying cosmological simulations. The diagnostic plots helped me assess the representation of selected galaxy properties and identify cases for closer inspection. The most useful comparisons connect the statistical shape of a model output to the astronomical variables it is meant to describe.

04 / RESEARCH

My contribution

I prepared the working datasets, modified the code used for conditional-flow experiments, ran training jobs, and examined the resulting galaxy-property distributions. This tutorial gave me practical experience moving between a scientific dataset, a machine-learning model, and the computational workflow required to test it.
