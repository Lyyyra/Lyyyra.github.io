01 / DESIGN

The design problem

A healthcare visit involves several tasks: understanding the next step, finding an appropriate appointment, locating the right place, and keeping track of visit information. In MediCamp, our five-person team designed an interface connecting these tasks in a single prototype. We considered how a person might move from describing a concern to scheduling and navigating a visit.

02 / DESIGN

Mapping the tasks

We began by describing representative users and the actions they would need to take. Personas and task-flow diagrams helped us map the decisions involved in triage, appointment booking, and navigation. We used the diagrams to decide which information should appear together and which parts of the process needed clearer transitions between screens.

03 / DESIGN

Building the prototype

We moved from early flows and wireframes to an interactive Figma prototype. Revisiting the task models as screens developed helped us adjust the interface's information hierarchy, navigation, and order of actions. The finished course prototype allows visitors to move through the proposed healthcare-visit experience and examine the relationships among its screens.

04 / DESIGN

My contribution

As project manager, I coordinated deadlines, milestones, and shared deliverables. I also contributed to the design documentation, information architecture, wireframes, and interface interactions. The project gave me practical experience coordinating design decisions across a team while developing a prototype that others could explore.
