Selected Work.

My research projects come first, from charged-dust simulations and galaxy modeling to astronomical data analysis and observing labs. The later projects cover HCI and game development. Each page describes the questions I worked on, the methods I used, and the parts I built or helped develop.

01 / RESEARCH↗

Charged Dust Dynamics

I build numerical simulations of charged dust grains around stars, changing grain properties and magnetic-field parameters to study escape, radial migration, and epicyclic motion.

Senior Thesis · Computational AstrophysicsOngoing senior thesis
02 / RESEARCH↗

DREAMS Cosmological Simulations

I prepared galaxy-simulation data, adapted code for conditional normalizing flows, ran training experiments on UVA's Rivanna cluster, and compared generated distributions with the simulation data.

Research Tutorial · Machine LearningCompleted research tutorial
03 / RESEARCH↗

Astrophysics Research Methods

Three analyses using SDSS, IllustrisTNG, and Gaia DR3: identifying Sagittarius Stream candidates, studying black hole–host correlations, and examining the stellar population and dynamics of NGC 362.

ASTR 4140 · Astronomical Data AnalysisCompleted undergraduate course research
04 / RESEARCH↗

Observational Astronomy

Five labs covering telescope and detector characterization, CCD image reduction, stellar photometry, RR Lyrae light curves, and wavelength-calibrated stellar spectra.
