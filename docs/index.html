01 / RESEARCH↗

Charged Dust Dynamics

I build orbital simulations to investigate how radiation, gravity, drag, and electromagnetic forces influence charged dust grains, including the conditions that produce escape, radial migration, or bounded oscillations.

Senior Thesis · Computational AstrophysicsOngoing senior thesis
02 / RESEARCH↗

DREAMS Cosmological Simulations

I worked with cosmological simulation data and conditional normalizing flows, adapting a machine-learning workflow, running experiments on UVA's Rivanna cluster, and comparing model-generated galaxy-property distributions with simulation data.

Astrophysics Research Methods

Three investigations using SDSS, IllustrisTNG, and Gaia DR3: identifying Sagittarius Stream candidates, measuring black hole–host scaling relations, and estimating the properties of globular cluster NGC 362.
