A Little About Me.

I study astronomy, write fiction, and stop to look at birds.

Hi, I'm Lyra! I study Astronomy and Computer Science at the University of Virginia, where I'm also working toward a minor in Philosophy. I like asking questions, following unexpected interests, and building things that take considerably longer than I first imagined.

I grew up reading encyclopedias and watching science documentaries. When I was eight, a documentary about Stephen Hawking drew me toward astronomy. During a family trip to Arxan in Inner Mongolia, I saw the Milky Way clearly for the first time, far from city lights. A few years later, I watched the Perseid meteor shower in Hongcun and decided that astronomy was what I wanted to study. In high school, I helped run our school's observatory, organized observing sessions, and taught other students to use telescopes.

I made my first very simple games in Scratch during elementary school, and college gave me a much deeper appreciation for programming. I enjoy the logic of designing a system and the creative satisfaction of making it work. Code gives me some of the same pleasure as writing fiction: an idea grows through structure, revision, and a great many decisions into something I can share with someone else. That's part of why I'm drawn to software development, game design, and HCI. Whenever I learn something fascinating, I tend to ask two questions: What else does this make me curious about? And how could I share it with other people?

Away from my desk, I write essays and fiction, take photographs, watch nature documentaries, play indie games, and spend an unreasonable amount of time looking at birds. Around UVA, I especially like the squirrels that get round in autumn, the groundhog near my residence hall, the crows nesting downstairs, the great blue heron fishing at Dell Pond, and the red-tailed hawks circling overhead in the late afternoon. Even a sparrow going about its day makes me happy. I like knowing that the world is full of lives carrying on quite independently of ours.

I'm also a feminist, a natural history enthusiast, and a fan of very large brunch omelets. An ideal free Saturday begins around lunchtime, includes writing or an indie game, and ends with strength training and matcha Häagen-Dazs. I maintain that ice cream eaten after a workout has no calories, since it's cold. My scientific training has yet to convince me otherwise.

DESIGN NOTE / THIS WEBSITE

How I Made This Website

This is actually my first time designing and building a website! I started by figuring out what kind of visual style I liked, then spent some time experimenting with color palettes, fonts, and different ways of arranging text and images. I eventually settled on six colors, Times New Roman, and a minimalist layout with geometric sections and thin dividing lines. From there, I kept adjusting the spacing, proportions, and composition as I worked on each page.

I was also learning web development along the way, from understanding how HTML and CSS work to organizing the site's structure and deploying it through GitHub Pages. There's been quite a bit of trial and error, and I'm still figuring things out as I continue adding new projects and making changes to the site.

I've really enjoyed the whole process, especially getting to experiment with both the visual design and the technical side of building something from scratch.
